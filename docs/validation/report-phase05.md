Generated by `tools/validation/run_validation.py` (data: `tools/validation/data/phase05_results.json`). Do not edit by hand. Measured data: BRAS (Aspöck, Brinkmann, Ackermann, Weinzierl, Vorländer; DOI 10.14279/depositonce-6726.3; CC BY-SA 4.0); this report is an adaptation under CC BY-SA 4.0.

**Read this first.** BRAS states that its complex rooms "cannot serve as a direct reference but may be used to compare the results from different simulations based on indentical input data" (as printed). All α and s are estimates. The errors below measure **method plus input data**; they are evidence on NFR-1 and AQ2, not a validation (researcher findings F2, F3, F6, F7). Strength G is **not evaluable**: the BRAS RIR levels were manually corrected by 6 dB (F3); T30, EDT and C80 do not depend on absolute level. Round-robin context (RR19, Brinkmann et al. 2019, JASA 145(4), DOI 10.1121/1.5096178): blind mature hybrids on the same initial data stayed above 1 jnd at 500 Hz–2 kHz in most cases; their per-room numbers were not retrieved in this session, so they are not printed beside ours.
