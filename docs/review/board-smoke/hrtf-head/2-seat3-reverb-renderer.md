(iii) Yaw following the centroid (AX3) rotates every image's head-relative direction, so a one-emitter move rebuilds the whole BRIR; that is the existing position class (AQ4/AX18 crossfade), dataset-independent. Barycentric interpolation is C0, not C1: slope changes at triangle edges are not clicks under crossfaded rebuilds.

5. **Evidence.**
- Time-domain interpolation of HRIRs with different onsets gives double peaks and spurious notches; minimum-phase or onset-removed magnitude interpolation with a separately inserted ITD solves it, transparent even at 60° spacing for non-individual HRTFs: Begault Ch. 4 "Problems with Interpolation Algorithms" (Figs. 4.39–4.42) `[Observed, corpus]`. Supports phase-08 scope 3 as written; prefer minimum-phase over raw onset-aligned HRIRs for the blend.
- HRTF filter lengths 128/256 typical; 72 taps without audible effect: Vorländer §9.4 `[Observed, corpus]`. Raw measurements usually 512–1024 taps at 44.1 kHz; rectangular windowing at a common point preserves ITD but can lose LF: Begault Ch. 4 "Methods for Data Reduction" `[corpus]`. 256 taps at 48 kHz = 5.3 ms is safe for the head; the risk is measurement-room content or LF extension past 5.3 ms cut by a hard window (spectral ripple). Per-set HRIR length `[Hyp]`, researcher R3.
- DF-EQ computed from the set removes the direction-independent part, including the ear-canal resonance (≈ 3 kHz) of eardrum-microphone measurement heads: Rumsey §3.2.4; Vorländer §6.3 outer ear `[corpus]`. So post-EQ colour differences between heads are the direction-dependent residual only; for the KU100, already near Theile's diffuse-field response (Rumsey §3.2.4; Begault Ch. 4), the EQ is near 0 dB. Whether the SOFA files hold raw or equalised data `[Hyp]`, R5; it matters little for this EQ, which is self-normalising.
- KU100 onset arrival spans ≈ −0.25 … 0.6 ms over azimuth (Zotter & Frank Fig. 4.25b); by symmetry the onset ITD at ±90° is ≈ 0.75–0.85 ms depending on the onset criterion (redone), against the sphere model 0.64 ms (R/c·(1 + π/2), R = 8.75 cm) and the human 650 µs (Blauert Ch. 2 Fig. 2.30). AC 3's 0.6–0.75 ms window may fail for the KU100 on an onset detector. Not my gate; flagged for R6 and Seat 1. For my seat any ITD value is a fractional delay.
- Rebuild numbers: AX22 (W1 20.6 ms, W2 28.5 ms, N_cap 1 560), AQ10 (50 ms, every output mode from Phase 08), AX3/AX18/AX33 `[project documents]`.
- Register: "artifacts on change are state discontinuities with known cures" (§19, Jaeger et al. 2023; Schlecht & Habets 2015) `[Observed]`.
Researcher: per set HRIR length, grid and lowest elevation, whether onset/minimum-phase processing or truncation was already applied, measurement-room reflections in the HRIR tails, left–right symmetry (Begault says the KU100's pinnae are symmetric; would halve storage).
