# Seat 8 — D-H3 (b)

SEAT 8 POSITION

Corpus present (c1b171f). Whose ears: a producer with no acoustics training puts the listener on a festival field or a mountainside (FR-3D-3), on headphones, and wants the space to say "outside, open, far"; the absence of a tail *is* her story (AX37 (5.4), my 5.4-A rating 1/1 in phase-05b2 file 3).

**Question 1.** `edge_values` (1.5 × 100 × 4 m slot, α alternating 0.01/1.0 per band, a −60 dB source 0.3 m from a listener in a corner and a +12 dB source 100 m away; from the code): no producer builds it; it is a scene nobody tells a story in. `open_field` (listener at 1.7 m, standing ear height; two sources 3 m up, 20 m off; from the code): yes, that *is* a producer's festival scene in shape. So the fixture reading costs me nothing, but open_field's outcome is the Phase 12 outdoor preset's outcome, and that is where my seat is touched.

**Question 2.** A field note reading "level unreliable; move either about 0.7 m away" tells her the space is faulty when it is doing exactly what she chose (dry, direct, a ground bounce). A ±2 dB note about a tail below −60 dB after t_dir + 50 ms (AX37 (5.4), plan id) is information about nothing she can hear: it is the tool's detector, not the audience's (RT4, RT8 `SRi, post`), and the complaint it provokes is Thom's "too much" (RT6 `SRg`). It also breaks criterion 3: the dry field fits the frame; the note invites her to "fix" it by pushing a tail up or moving the stage off the ground.

## D-H3 (e)

SEAT 8 POSITION (narrow)

Whose ears: a producer auditioning a 100 m hall on headphones, in the tweak-and-press-play loop, who commits the scene later by freeze or bounce-in-place (§8 speed requirement; my phase-05b2 file 23 premortem).

1. **Reading.** A transport reset in a very large room can either stall the whole session (0.2–2.6 s, host-dependent, only when the IR is stale, i.e. right after a tweak: §0 row 07, convene file K-e10, measurement) or play silent until the scene "settles". Cue: GF3/GF7, the technology must recede.

**The two experiences.** The stall lands exactly on the audition loop that the plugin is for: tweak the room, press play, the DAW hangs. She cannot tell it from a crash; she learns "big rooms are heavy" and either avoids the arena, festival and cathedral points of view (criterion 1) or freezes early to escape the stalls, which commits the scene before she has judged contrast and depth (criteria 2, 4) and removes her ability to move sources. The silent start is a known DAW idiom (samplers streaming, convolution loading): transport runs, the rest of the session plays, "settling" explains it (AX38 (iii)), the track fades in. Freeze itself is unchanged under every option (offline rebuilds before the block, X11), and a bounce that starts identically every time is what my seat needs from a commit. **Manual:** I would print "after a room edit in a very large room the first play may take a few seconds to settle; the track fades in when the room is ready". I would not print "may pause your DAW for up to 2.6 s".
