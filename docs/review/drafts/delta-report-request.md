Dear Dr Prawda,

I am developing a room-acoustic simulation plugin and would like to use your Arni dataset (Zenodo
10.5281/zenodo.6985104) as a validation reference, with attribution under its CC BY 4.0 licence.
Thank you for publishing it, and the combination-to-panel mapping in particular.

Your JASA 2022 paper (Table II, ref. 64) takes the absorption coefficients of the open and closed
panels from a DELTA / FORCE Technology report, "Exploratory measurement of sound absorption
coefficient for variable acoustic panel" (2018). The link given for it
(flexac.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/117-36347-Flex-Acoustics_Variable-acoustic-module_TC-101192.pdf)
no longer resolves.

Could you share a copy of that report, or tell me:

1. the measurement standard used (for example ISO 354 in a reverberation room), and the specimen
   area and mounting;
2. the frequency range and band resolution of the published coefficients;
3. any stated measurement uncertainty.

I would also be grateful to know whether the wall and floor coefficients used for the room were
ever measured, and what the 5.75 × 7.87 × 2.91 m outline in Fig. 1 of McKenzie et al. (2021)
refers to, compared with the 8.9 × 6.3 × 3.6 m room in your paper.

I would cite the report and your dataset in our validation documentation.

Kind regards,
Maisara Abourady
