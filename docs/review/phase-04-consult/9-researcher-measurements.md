Researcher report for Phase 05 (BRAS coverage, σ₁,Δ, ΔL_b, ray-tracer bias, and the NOT CHECKABLE items). Accessed 2026-11-05. All tools were read-only and no repo files were changed. Downloaded PDFs are in the session tool-results folder only. Nothing was committed.

## 1. Questions examined
- **R1.** BRAS contents: rooms, geometry, α/s data, measured parameters, atmosphere, licence, uncertainty. Which rooms reduce to a shoebox, classified by contrast and s.
- **R2.** A-priori scatter of EDT and C80 (single realisation, or measurement uncertainty), to judge whether R > 2 000 per arm is plausible.
- **R3.** How much EDT, C80 and level change when scattering is added (ISM-only vs hybrid), including the round robins.
- **R4.** Bias of stochastic ray tracing against radiosity or analytic references.
- **R5.** The √V-ms mixing time; whether ISO 9613-1 rests on Bass 1995; the accuracy of Fitzroy and Arau-Puchades; ground scattering.

Corpus check first: `docs/corpus` has nothing on BRAS. It covers the round robins only qualitatively (Vorländer §11.5, §11.6), the ray-tracing statistics (Vorländer §11.2.5 Eqs. 11.7–11.28), the noise-level variance (Kuttruff Eqs. 8.40–8.41) and the Table 5.2 iteration results (Kuttruff §5.7). Everything else below is from outside the corpus.

### R2 arithmetic (derived from the phase-05 rule, not from a source)
R > 2 000 per arm happens when σ₁,Δ > √(2000/144) = **3.73 jnd** (room-averaged), or σ₁,Δ,room or σ₁,Δ,pair > √(2000/9) = **14.9 jnd**.

For C80 (jnd 1 dB) the paired-difference SD would have to exceed 3.7 dB. For EDT (jnd 5 %) it would have to exceed about 19 %. F18 puts unpaired single-realisation late-energy scatter at about 0.4 dB at 1 kHz and 1–2 dB at 63–125 Hz for T = 1 s. Pairing with the same seeds should reduce it further. On this indirect evidence, **R > 2 000 looks implausible in the AQ9 bands. A risk remains only in the lowest bands for EDT**, where no source gives a value.

## 4. Conflicts between sources
- **Does EDT change with s?** SH11 (CATT, one hall) says scattering has "no influence" on EDT, although its Fig. 3 shows about +3 %. The SHJ15 abstract (three programs) reports EDT rising with s. Both agree that C80 falls with s.
- **√V constant.** LKW12 attributes t_mp ≈ √V ms to a 400 s⁻¹ threshold, but its own Eq. 1 gives 0.89·√V ms at 400 s⁻¹. 1.0·√V corresponds to 507 s⁻¹ (derived).

## 6. For the decider: facts you can rely on
- **F1, F2:** BRAS has only 4 rooms (CR1–CR4), and its authors say they "cannot serve as a direct reference".
- **F3:** documented V, temperature and RH per room. CR1 is coupled, so effectively at most 3 single-volume rooms. All CR RIRs carry a manual 6 dB level correction.
- **F4:** no dimensions or S in the documentation; they must come from the .skp files.
- **F6:** all CR α are estimated. The fitted set is one per-band factor for all materials per scene, so it keeps the absorption contrast.
- **F7:** all s are estimated by s = 0.5√(d_char·f/c), clamped to [0.05, 0.99], always rising with f.
- **F5, F13:** shoebox reduction of CR2–CR4 has been done before (RAZR). Blind mature hybrids missed T by 58 % / 35 % at 125 / 250 Hz and stayed above 1 jnd at 500 Hz–2 kHz in most cases.
- **F15:** the full s swing (0.1 → 0.9) moved room-averaged EDT and C80 by about 0.6 jnd in one CATT study. An order-of-magnitude prior for Δ̂.
- **F16:** with strong contrast and low s, T can vary about tenfold with s (2D idealisation).
- **F18 with the R2 arithmetic:** R > 2 000 needs σ₁,Δ > 3.7 jnd. Noise-tail level scatter is about 0.4 dB at 1 kHz, so this is unlikely in the AQ9 bands. The low-band EDT risk is unquantified.
- **F20:** perceptual mixing time t_mp50 = 0.58√V + 21.2 ms, or 20·V/S + 12 ms (LKW12 Eqs. 12–13).
- **F21:** ISO 9613-1:1993 cannot be based on Bass et al. 1995.
