## Researcher report: candidate second validation datasets (PRD Q9; decider report 11, F1–F3)

I read the role file, decider report 11 and my earlier report 9 (F14 on RR-III). The corpus holds no dataset documentation, so everything below comes from web sources. All were accessed 2026-11-05. "Read" means I opened the paper or record text myself. "Derived" means my own arithmetic (V = L·W·H). I edited no repo files.

### 1. Question examined
Which openly available measured RIR datasets could be a second validation source for a shoebox ISM plus statistical-tail simulator? The decider's criteria are: shoebox-like rooms, measured materials, rooms under 100 m³, and a licence that allows commercial use.

### 3. Not found / unresolved
- **No dataset found with in-situ or lab-measured α (and s) for all surfaces of a small shoebox, together with measured IRs and a commercial licence.** Searches: "in situ absorption" + RIR dataset, "impedance tube" / "reverberation chamber" + room IR dataset + geometry, Zenodo/DepositOnce/Mendeley. A data paper that pairs ISO 354 / ISO 10534 / in-situ p-u data with ISO 3382 IRs in one room would settle it.
- **Arni:** the DELTA 2018 panel report, and whether the 2022 JASA paper publishes calibrated α. The full text returned 403.
- **SRIRACHA:** the α data and the licence. The Acta Acustica full text returned 403; `license.txt` was not shown.
- **RR-III:** studio volume, IR availability and licence. The IOA PDFs returned 404; the XLS files were not opened.
- **OpenAIR:** the site returned 503. A search summary says licences are set per entry (some CC BY 4.0), but I could not check any entry, so nothing is reported as a finding.
- **Treble and other synthetic sets (HARP, MB-RIRs, GWA):** not examined in detail. They are simulated, so they cannot serve as measured references.
- **Brinkmann et al. round-robin rooms:** these are the BRAS CR rooms (report 9, F13). There is no separate dataset.
- **May et al. 2025** (Acoustics 7(3):56, CC BY): a racquetball court used as a "simple shoebox". Whether its data are published was not checked. Lead only.
- **RSoANU, ISOBEL, AIR:** room tables not retrieved.

### 4. Conflicts between sources
- **C1 Arni dimensions.** The SMC 2020 paper (§3.1) and the Aalto portal give 8.9 × 6.3 × 3.6 m, which derives to 202 m³. McKenzie et al. (Fig. 1) give 5.75 × 7.87 × 2.91 m, which derives to 132 m³. The difference is unexplained; it may be the free interior between panels and curtains.
- **C2 SRIRACHA licence.** The DepositOnce item links CC BY-SA 4.0. The acoupipe documentation states CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- **C3 ACE licence.** Zenodo 6257551 gives CC BY 4.0. IEEE DataPort says CC BY-ND 4.0.
- **C4 Arni source.** The SMC 2020 measurements used directional Genelec 8030A. The 2022 Zenodo set says "omnidirectional sound source". They may be different campaigns.

### 6. For the decider
- **No candidate meets every criterion.** None has measured α for all surfaces of a room under 100 m³ (D1–D16, §3).
- **Arni (D1)** is the only shoebox with lab-measured α for its variable surfaces under a commercial licence (CC BY 4.0). It is above 100 m³ (132–202 m³, C1), its other surfaces use textbook α, and the source report is not public.
- **Motus (D2)** is a shoebox of about 60 m³ with a 3D model per configuration and α per surface (textbook values), under CC BY 4.0. It has the same input-data limitation as BRAS.
- **dEchorate (D3)** is a cuboid of 86.4 m³ with controlled, per-face reflective or absorbing switching, under CC BY 4.0. It has measured RT for 500 Hz–4 kHz and documented T/RH, but no α.
- **Other commercial-licence options under 100 m³ without material data:** SoundCam (MIT; people in the room), BUT ReverbDB L207 (CC BY 4.0), and SRIRACHA, ACE and RR-III with their licences unresolved (C2, C3, D9).
- **Excluded on licence:** C4DM (NC-SA). MIT Survey is also excluded (no geometry, unconfirmed licence).
- **Fit ranking (my reading of the stated criteria, not a recommendation):**
  1. Arni
  2. Motus
  3. dEchorate
  4. SRIRACHA (if the licence is resolved)
  5. SoundCam
  6. BUT ReverbDB
  7. ACE
  8. MeshRIR
  9. RR-III
  10. the rest
- **Before relying on Arni or the Pilot Studio set (D12),** open their data files to confirm α provenance. That is the step that could raise "measured materials" from lead to fact.
