### 4. Conflicts between sources
- **C1 (resolved in part).** JASA 2022 §V.A says 8.9 × 6.3 × 3.6 m with V = 201.9 m³. McKenzie et al. Fig. 1 says 5.75 × 7.87 × 2.91 m. The JASA value is the stated room volume. The other figure is unexplained.
- **C4 (resolved).** The SMC 2020 campaign used Genelec 8030A. The JASA 2022 campaign used a 01dB LS01 omni. These are different campaigns. The SRIR set used Genelec 8331A.
- **C5 (new).** The number of Arni combinations is K = 5312 in JASA 2022 (§V.A, §VI.D) and 5 342 on the Zenodo 6985104 record.
- **C6 (new).** The Mendeley record calls the room a "Pilot Studio", but its only link is to the lecture hall paper, and its image shows tiered seating. It is not certain that the "studio" is that hall.

### 6. For the decider
- **Arni volume is 201.9 m³ (8.9 × 6.3 × 3.6 m),** as stated in a peer-reviewed paper (V5). It is above 100 m³.
- **Arni α are not all measured (V1–V3).** The panel α come from one "exploratory" DELTA industry report that is not retrievable and names no standard. Walls and floor use textbook values, and the curtain uses manufacturer data. The only calibrated values are room-average β + nΔ, fitted to RT, and they appear in a figure, not a table.
- **Arni 2022 dataset (V6):** the panel state of every IR is in a CSV, under CC BY 4.0. Positions exist only as a dimensioned drawing, and the atmospheric conditions are documented (V4).
- **Arni SRIR dataset (V7):** exact coordinates are tabulated, but the configurations are given only as a % of absorbers.
- **The Bologna "Pilot Studio" is not a candidate (V8, V9).** The record holds only a one-page call, and the data must be requested by email. The associated room is a lecture hall of about 900 m³ with tiered seating, not a shoebox. Its α were calibrated to measured T30, not measured.
- **May et al. (V11)** is a true shoebox of 6.1 × 12.2 × 6.1 m (≈ 454 m³). Its measured RIRs are on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0. Its α are ODEON library values, and the mic is a TetraMic.
- **Report 13's main conclusion still stands.** No candidate pairs measured α for all surfaces with measured IRs in a shoebox under 100 m³ under a commercial licence.
